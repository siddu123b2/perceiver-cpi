04 / One pair. More perspectives.

Discovery starts
with a pair.

Predict affinity with your dataset model, then explore the matching protein, compound and pocket in three dimensions.

The score and structure are shown separately, with their sources and limitations.

Docking predicts a protein structure locally with ESMFold (maximum 1,022 residues), ranks pockets with P2Rank, and docks the compound with AutoDock Vina. First use downloads about 16.9 GB of model weights. This laptop has 4 GB GPU memory; disk offload may be slow or may not fit. Predicted structures, pocket probabilities, and Vina scores are not experimental evidence or measured affinity.
